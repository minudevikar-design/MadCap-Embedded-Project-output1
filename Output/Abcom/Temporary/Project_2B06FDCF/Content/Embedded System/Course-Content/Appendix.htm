Reading the Similarity Rating

The similarity rating feature, which can be accessed on the side right panel in Turnitin, detects the use of outside sources in a student’s assignment. This tool detects content from websites and from other students’ submitted work.

 

This feature requires careful consideration. If the similarity rating is high, this may indicate that much of the students’ work is not his/her own and may be an academic integrity violation.

However, the similarity may also be properly cited material or the result of the student using a StraighterLine template. Because of this, you cannot flag/place an AHP Account Hold on an assignment just because it has a high similarity match. Instead, you must look at the matching content and, using your best judgment, discern whether the content that matches is plagiarism.

 

Below are some reminders for using the similarity match feature. Please access “Interpreting a Similarity Report” from the Turnitin website to understand the full considerations.

 

  1. The Similarity Percentage can be accessed by clicking the top box on the right panel of the submission (will likely include the percentage or a “0%”).

How to Place an Account Hold

 

  1. After determining that the assignment needs to receive an Account Hold, insert a QuickMark or comment indicating why a hold needs to be placed on the account. Keep

 

in mind that your comment is visible to the student, so you should ensure that your tone isn’t accusatory or demeaning in any way.

Note: If the assignment is being placed on hold for a Similarity Match, insert a QuickMark from “Academic Integrity,” “The Plagiarism Spectrum,” or “New Science Lab Marks: Similarity Match” lists. (Please familiarize yourself with these marks and choose the one that appropriately fits the situation.)
