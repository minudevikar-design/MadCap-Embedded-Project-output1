Physics Science Lab Grading Procedure Updates

 

A New Focus

Previously, submissions were flagged/placed on hold if aspects of the assignment were missing and/or incorrect. Now, all assignments will be given ample review, substantive feedback, and a grade unless otherwise noted below. This will cut down on unnecessary resubmissions and grading delays and align our grading policies more closely with those of other higher-education institutions. If assignments do contain issues, we will continue to place detailed QuickMarks in the assignment so that the reason for an Account Hold is visible to the student. However, the major change is that the majority of the assignment will still be graded, even if placing an account hold is required.
